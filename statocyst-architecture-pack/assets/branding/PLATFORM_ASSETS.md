# Platform Assets

These exports were rebuilt from the corrected approved Statocyst master icon.

## Design anchor

All platform assets should preserve:

- the neon-glass jellyfish silhouette,
- the **central pearl / bubble**,
- the sonar-wave lines beneath the pearl,
- the dark rounded-square primary background for opaque icons.

## Asset groups

- `platform-icons/common/` — generic PNG sizes.
- `platform-icons/windows/` — Windows PNGs plus `statocyst.ico`.
- `platform-icons/macos/` — macOS PNG sizes plus `statocyst.icns`.
- `platform-icons/linux/` — Linux PNG sizes.
- `platform-icons/ios/` — opaque app icon exports.
- `platform-icons/android/` — Android launcher icons plus adaptive foreground/background/monochrome assets.
- `transparent/` — transparent symbol sizes for compositing and adaptive use.

## Notes

- iOS icons are opaque by design.
- Transparent/adaptive assets are derived from the approved primary icon and should receive a final visual review before release.
- The contact sheet and preview materials in this folder are generated from the corrected assets and intended as packaging references.
