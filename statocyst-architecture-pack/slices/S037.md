# S037 — Durable transfers and a real Downloads screen

**Milestone:** Rural offline
**Status:** PARTIAL
**Depends on:** S032

## Objective
Make transfers survive reboot, process death and Android's foreground-service limits, and
give downloads a permanent place in the UI.

## Scope and boundaries
- WorkManager with unmetered/charging constraints; reschedule after the Android 15 6-hour cap.
- Foreground service only while a transfer is active.
- Keep `.part` on cancel with a TTL; jittered backoff; global concurrency limit.
- Downloads screen: per-item pause/resume/retry/size/quality/delete, undo.
- Replaces the floating download card as the primary surface.

## Acceptance gate
- Reboot mid-queue and the transfer resumes.
- The six-hour cap is simulated and the work is rescheduled.
- Cancel then undo restores the item; per-item state is accurate.
- Evidence in `evidence/S037-report.md`.
