# S033 — Playback that never gives up

**Milestone:** Rural offline
**Status:** PLANNED
**Depends on:** —

## Objective
Hold playback at the current position through a long dead zone and resume automatically
when the network returns.

## Scope and boundaries
- Replace the fixed eight-attempt recovery with an indefinite offline hold.
- Keep the item and position; do not skip on network loss.
- `onAvailable`/reachability wakes the retry; re-resolve on Wi-Fi↔LTE and server failover.
- Capped backoff with jitter; no polling loop and no wake-lock spam.
- Surface "Offline — will resume at M:SS" in the player bar, Now Playing and notification.
- iOS equivalent in `PlaybackManager`.

## Acceptance gate
- A 10-minute outage resumes at the same position without user action.
- A handover re-resolves the source without skipping.
- The queue does not advance on recoverable errors.
- Instrumented or unit coverage for the state machine; iOS build compiles.

## Required execution steps
1. Model the states (`playing | buffering | waiting-network | error`) and tests.
2. Implement Android and iOS.
3. Verify with a shaped network.
4. Record evidence in `evidence/S033-report.md`.
