# S034 — iOS offline parity

**Milestone:** Rural offline
**Status:** IN_PROGRESS
**Depends on:** S031, S033

## Objective
Bring iOS transfers and playback to Android's rural behaviour, not just its feature list.

## Scope and boundaries
- Background `URLSession` with resume data and a persistent pending queue.
- Range resume, retry/backoff, progress events, cancellation that keeps `.part`.
- Playback recovery and adaptive quality actually applied.
- Persist the download queue; restore after launch and after background suspension.
- No CarPlay UI here (S039).

## Acceptance gate
- Kill the app mid-download; relaunch resumes from the partial file.
- A 10-minute outage recovers playback at the same position.
- Progress is reported during the transfer, not only at completion.
- The background session survives at least two minutes with the app suspended.
- iOS CI build passes; device evidence is recorded or the gate is BLOCKED.

## Required execution steps
1. Move transfers to a background session with a delegate and resume data.
2. Persist pending ids and profile metadata.
3. Add tests for resume/backoff where a host-side harness is possible.
4. Build in CI; record device evidence or the exact blocker.
5. Record evidence in `evidence/S034-report.md`.
