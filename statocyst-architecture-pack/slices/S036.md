# S036 — Plan-ahead prefetch and collection pins

**Milestone:** Rural offline
**Status:** PARTIAL
**Depends on:** S030, S031

## Objective
Let a user make an album, playlist or artist available offline in one action, with the
size known first and enough lead time before a trip.

## Scope and boundaries
- Bitrate-aware prefetch horizon (next 10 minutes or the whole collection) within budget.
- Prefetch on Wi-Fi/charging; pause on metered.
- Album/playlist/artist "Make offline" sheet with quality and size.
- Coverage badges ("12 of 14 offline"); artwork prefetch.
- No new storage engine (uses S031 pins and S035 cache).

## Acceptance gate
- A 12-track album is made offline in one action; the sheet shows size before starting.
- All tracks play offline and artwork renders offline.
- The action is idempotent and reports partial failure per track.

## Required execution steps
1. Add the coverage/estimate pure model with tests.
2. Add the UI and queue wiring.
3. Verify on a shaped network and an offline device.
4. Record evidence in `evidence/S036-report.md`.
