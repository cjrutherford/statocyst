# S055 — Video enclosure parsing and primary/alternate selection

**Milestone:** MVP-6 Video podcasts
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED (parser suite/typecheck pass; persistence integration covered by desktop tests)
**Depends on:** S054

## Objective

Teach the shared feed parser and ingestion pipeline to recognize video and audio/video
alternates, persist them, and choose a primary presentation, without touching playback or
UI.

## Scope and boundaries

- Parse every valid enclosure rather than the first: RSS `<enclosure>`, `media:content`
  (`medium`, `type`, `width`, `height`, `bitrate`), Atom enclosure links and existing
  Podcast 2.0 link representations.
- Classify form from MIME first, then `medium`, then a conservative URL/extension hint.
  `application/octet-stream` is not assumed video.
- Primary selection: audio preferred when both exist; video primary when only video; the
  feed's `defaultMediaForm` can prefer video. Retain at most 8 alternates, deduplicated,
  with the primary not repeated.
- Use the existing HTTP(S)/SSRF validation for every enclosure. Only publisher HTTP(S)
  URLs are accepted; no platform pseudo-URLs.
- Persist `media_form`, `alternates_json`, `poster_url`, `video_width`/`video_height` and
  feed `default_media_form` in the existing feed-refresh transaction.
- Update the fixture feed corpus and parser tests. No UI, protocol or engine work.
- No duration-based classification: a video episode is video because the feed declares a
  video enclosure.

## Acceptance gate

- Video-only, audio-only, both-forms, multiple-enclosure, relative-URL, malformed-MIME,
  oversized-alternates and SSRF fixtures all behave as specified.
- An audio+video feed produces an audio primary and a video alternate; a video-only feed
  produces a video primary; no episode is duplicated.
- `media_item.media_form` and `podcast_episode.media_form` agree with the persisted primary.
- Poster selection uses episode art first, feed art second, and a landscape
  `media:thumbnail` when present; failed art is non-fatal.
- `media:content` width/height/bitrate are preserved when available.
- Refresh remains idempotent and transactional; existing episodes keep resume/played state.
- Parser and persistence suites pass; no UI or playback tests change.

## Required execution steps

1. Extend parsed-episode types and fixtures; write failing parser tests first.
2. Implement enclosure collection, form classification and primary selection in
   `libs/podcast-core`.
3. Extend `toEpisodeUpsert`/repository writes and the media-item mapping.
4. Add poster/size metadata passthrough and a bounded alternates serializer test.
5. Re-run the existing podcast parser/repository suites and typecheck.
6. Record evidence in `evidence/S055-report.md`.

## Completion rule

VERIFIED only when the fixture corpus and a real feed sample produce the expected primary,
alternates and metadata with no regression in audio-only feeds.
