# S058 — iOS video presentation, PiP and background audio

**Milestone:** MVP-6 Video podcasts
**Status:** PLANNED
**Depends on:** S054, S055

## Objective

Play video podcast episodes on iOS through the existing AVPlayer, with a full-screen
`AVPlayerLayer`, Picture-in-Picture and audio that continues when the app is backgrounded or
the screen is locked.

## Scope and boundaries

- Keep the existing `PlaybackManager`/AVPlayer as the only player and the
  `AVAudioSession.playback` session as the only audio session.
- Add a full-screen video surface (`AVPlayerLayer`) launched from Now Playing, with poster,
  aspect-fit and rotation handling.
- Add `AVPictureInPictureController` where the OS supports it; detach the layer on
  background/audio-only/screen-off while audio continues.
- Update `MPNowPlayingInfoCenter`/`MPRemoteCommandCenter` for the form and existing
  play/pause/seek/speed/skip actions.
- iOS build exposes **no** YouTube companion or extraction surface. RSS video only.
- No schema work (S054) and no podcast parser work (S055).
- CarPlay remains audio-only; no video templates.

## Acceptance gate

- A video podcast fixture plays full-screen through the existing queue; play/pause/seek,
  rate and skip behave as in MVP-5.
- Backgrounding during video detaches the layer and continues audio; returning restores the
  video at the same position.
- Locking the screen continues audio for at least 60 minutes; playback controls work from
  the lock screen/Control Center.
- PiP works on a supported simulator/device, or the limitation is recorded as BLOCKED with
  a reproduction recipe.
- The YouTube/companion entry point is absent from the iOS build; a binary inspection note
  confirms no extraction dependency.
- Audio-only podcasts, music and CarPlay show no regression.
- Evidence includes a screen recording, lock-screen screenshot and PiP evidence.

## Required execution steps

1. Add the video surface controller and presentation plumbing to the plugin.
2. Wire lifecycle detach/attach and audio-session behavior; do not create a second player.
3. Add PiP configuration and capability reporting.
4. Extend now-playing info and remote commands.
5. Run Xcode build, unit tests and a simulator/device scenario; record any BLOCKED gate.
6. Confirm the iOS target has no companion code or extraction dependency.
7. Record evidence in `evidence/S058-report.md`.

## Completion rule

VERIFIED only with screen-off audio, background/foreground surface behavior and PiP
evidence on a named iOS simulator/device. App Store distribution remains a separate gate.
