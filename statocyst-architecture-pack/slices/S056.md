# S056 — Desktop video presentation and background playback

**Milestone:** MVP-6 Video podcasts
**Status:** PLANNED
**Depends on:** S054, S055

## Objective

Play video podcast enclosures on Electron desktop with a real video surface, audio-only
mode, PiP/fullscreen and background audio that survives minimize, workspace switch and
window close-to-tray.

## Scope and boundaries

- Renderer: a media-element manager that owns one `<audio>` or `<video>` at a time; video
  stage in Now Playing with poster fallback; Play video / Audio only toggle, PiP and
  fullscreen buttons; no second player in the mini player.
- Engine: consume `mediaForm` and `posterUrl`; report video-ready state; keep existing
  token/seek/rate/volume semantics. `setPresentation` reloads at the current position only
  when the form changes.
- Protocol: allow video MIME types through `statocyst-media:`; keep Range/HEAD semantics;
  serve video enclosures with the same redirect and availability mapping.
- Main: `backgroundThrottling: false` for the main window, `powerSaveBlocker` only while
  playing, `navigator.mediaSession` metadata/actions in the renderer, and a settings-driven
  close-to-tray behavior that does not quit playback unexpectedly.
- Settings: video preference (`auto`/`audio-only`/`ask`), data-saver preference, PiP
  availability. Persist only preferences, not presentation of a completed episode.
- No native mobile surfaces and no third-party extractor.

## Acceptance gate

- A local video podcast fixture plays with video visible; seeking, rate, volume, pause and
  resume work through the existing authoritative queue.
- Audio-only hides the surface and keeps audio playing; switching back restores the video at
  the same position.
- Minimizing the window, switching workspaces and locking the session do not stop audio;
  the power-save blocker is released on pause/end/error.
- PiP and fullscreen work where Chromium supports them; unsupported controls are hidden,
  not broken.
- Media keys play/pause/next/previous through the existing queue.
- An audio-only podcast and a music track behave exactly as before; no music regression.
- Playwright Electron E2E covers play → video visible → audio-only → minimize → resume →
  restart. Evidence includes a screenshot of the video stage and the background state.

## Required execution steps

1. Add the media-element manager and contract consumption with unit tests.
2. Add the video stage, poster fallback and controls; keep keyboard/ARIA behavior.
3. Wire protocol video MIME and test Range/HEAD against the fixture.
4. Add power-save/media-session/background behavior and settings.
5. Extend the E2E harness with a video fixture feed and the background scenario.
6. Run typecheck, unit/E2E suites and a packaged-app smoke if the slice changes packaging.
7. Record evidence in `evidence/S056-report.md`.

## Completion rule

VERIFIED only with real Electron playback (not jsdom), background audio observed, and the
existing music/audio-podcast E2E still passing.
